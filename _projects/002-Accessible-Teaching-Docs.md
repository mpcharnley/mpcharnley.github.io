With the new ADA Title II ruling requiring all material published on public entity websites to meet accessibility standards, there has been a push to make teaching documents, particularly course notes, meet this standard. Below is a collection of things that I have tried, learned, or found that could help others on this mission.

Attempts
===
1. LaTeX Tagging Project and accessible PDFs.
2. Direct use of Canvas Pages using LaTeXML.
3. PreTeXt and embedded SCORM files in Canvas.
